Mars Fact Sheet

Equatorial Radius 3,389.5 km
Surface Gravity 3.72 m/s² (0.38g)
Distance to Sun 227.9 M km (1.52 AU)
Orbital Period 687 Earth days
Surface Temp -153°C to 20°C
Known Moons 2 (Phobos, Deimos)
Atmosphere 95% CO₂, Thin

Mars is the fourth planet from the Sun. Its signature red color comes from iron oxide (rust) on its surface.

Despite being a freezing desert today, Mars features massive extinct volcanoes like Olympus Mons and a grand canyon system, Valles Marineris, suggesting a highly active and potentially watery past.
