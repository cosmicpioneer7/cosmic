Mercury Explorer

The Scorched Planet
The smallest and innermost planet in the Solar System, heavily cratered and baked by the Sun.

Mercury Fact Sheet

Equatorial Radius 2,439.7 km
Surface Gravity 3.7 m/s² (0.38g)
Distance to Sun 58 Million km (0.39 AU)
Orbital Period 88 Earth days
Surface Temp -173°C to 427°C
Known Moons None
Atmosphere Exosphere (Trace)

Mercury is a rocky, barren world resembling our Moon. Because it is so close to the Sun and lacks an insulating atmosphere, it experiences the most extreme temperature fluctuations in the solar system.

Despite its proximity to the Sun, deep craters at Mercury's poles permanently shadow regions of water ice.
