Earth Fact Sheet

Equatorial Radius 6,378.1 km
Surface Gravity 9.807 m/s² (1g)
Distance to Sun 149.6 M km (1 AU)
Orbital Period 365.25 days
Surface Temp -89°C to 58°C
Estimated Age ~4.54 billion years
Atmosphere 78% N₂, 21% O₂

Earth is the third planet from the Sun and the only astronomical object known to harbor life. Over 71% of its surface is covered in water, giving it its vibrant blue appearance.

It orbits the Sun amidst a complex and dynamic atmosphere, which protects life by absorbing harmful solar radiation and regulating temperature.
