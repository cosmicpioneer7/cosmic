The Ice Giant
The seventh planet from our Sun, rolling on its side through the dark edge of the solar system.

Uranus Fact Sheet

Equatorial Radius 25,362 km
Surface Gravity 8.69 m/s² (0.886g)
Distance to Sun 2.9 Billion km (19.2 AU)
Orbital Period 84 Earth years
Cloud Top Temp -224°C
Known Moons 28 recognized
Atmosphere H₂, He, Methane

Uranus is a unique ice giant with a pale cyan hue caused by methane in its upper atmosphere absorbing red light.

Most notably, Uranus has an extreme axial tilt of 97.77 degrees. This causes the planet and its faint, narrow ring system to appear as if it is rolling on its side as it orbits the Sun.
